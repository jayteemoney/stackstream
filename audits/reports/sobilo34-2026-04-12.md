## Findings

### L-7 — Non-zero rate-per-block invariant violated for tiny deposits

**Severity:** Low (real edge case)

**Issue:** The documented invariant states that `rate-per-block` cannot be zero given `deposit > 0` and `duration > 0`. With Clarity integer division, `rate-per-block = floor((deposit * PRECISION) / duration)` is zero whenever `(deposit * PRECISION) < duration`.

**Effects when rate is zero:**
- No accrual for the recipient (claim always returns zero).
- `top-up-stream` divides by `rate` and fails for those streams.
- Funds are recoverable by the sender via `cancel-stream` (no theft), but the implied invariant is broken and the stream is effectively dead-on-arrival.

**Recommendation:** Reject `create-stream` when `(deposit-amount * PRECISION) < duration-blocks` (equivalently, require `>=` so the computed rate is at least 1).

**Resolution:** Guard added in `create-stream`. See current `stream-manager.clar:233–235`:

```clarity
;; Prevent zero rate-per-block: deposit * PRECISION must be >= duration-blocks
(asserts! (>= (* deposit-amount PRECISION) duration-blocks) ERR-INVALID-DURATION)
```

A regression test was added in `tests/stream-manager.test.ts` covering the edge case.

**Triage classification:** Fixed-on-main.

## Residual Notes

The auditor flagged the following operational considerations (not bugs):

- **Operator must protect `CONTRACT-OWNER`.** A compromised admin key can pause the protocol. We have since added two-step ownership transfer (`propose-ownership` / `accept-ownership`) as additional defense.
- **Emergency pause scope matches code** (manager-level circuit breaker, not factory).
- **Third-party token behavior remains a deployment/integration risk.** The contract verifies token principal equality and trusts the SIP-010 trait; malicious or non-conformant token implementations are outside the contract's control. Frontend whitelists trusted tokens.
