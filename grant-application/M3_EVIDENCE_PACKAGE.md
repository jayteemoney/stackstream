### Correction to the Oct 9 record

The Oct 9 version of this package recorded the creator test as "5 creators, 20 USDA each over 7 days, funded by ZADAO from its own wallet, starting Oct 12". What actually happened is different in four ways, and the earlier line is superseded by this section.

1. The streams were opened on Oct 19, not Oct 12.
2. Each carries 1.00 USDA, not 20 USDA. The budget for the test was cut to about six US dollars in total, so the amount per creator was cut with it.
3. The 5.00 USDA came from StackStream, not from the organisation. The organisation's wallet paid it out to the five recipients.
4. The streams have no memo set on them. The plan was for each to carry a memo saying it was a sponsored test, and the memo was never written. The sponsorship is therefore stated here, and it should be stated in every public mention of the test.

The seven-day figure was the plan: the app assumes five-second blocks, and 120,960 blocks at five seconds is seven days. The chain is running at about 9.8 seconds per block, so the same 120,960 blocks take about 13.7 days. The streams behave exactly as coded. The app's block-time constant is what is wrong, and it is recorded as a product fix in Section 8 rather than quietly corrected in the numbers.

## Section 8, What is missing, and the three things that would change the numbers

Nothing in this section is a claim. Each item is a gap that the Oct 21 relaunch is built around, and each one is stated the same way to the grant team as it is stated here.

**1. There is no protocol-wide "total value streamed" number.** The milestone asks for $10,000 equivalent streamed, and the chain cannot answer that question today. The contract tracks deposits and rates per stream, and the front end sums what it can see, but there is no single on-chain aggregate and no USD or STX equivalent applied across tokens. Until that exists, this package reports raw token amounts, which is the honest unit: 13.90 USDA across our own test streams and 5.00 USDA across the organisation's five streams.

**2. Streams created outside the app are not linked to the organisation record.** Four of the five creator streams were opened from the organisation's wallet and are not linked to its organisation record. That single gap is why the headline count is 0 rather than 1. Linking is a one-call action in the contract, so the fix is a flow, not a rewrite: when a stream is opened by a registered admin wallet, the organisation record is updated in the same transaction.

**3. The app's block-time constant is wrong.** The app assumes five-second blocks. Mainnet is running at about 9.8 seconds per block. Any duration a user picks in the app is therefore about twice as long as the app says it is, and the same bug is why the creator test runs 13.7 days instead of 7. Every duration in the interface needs to be computed from the real chain cadence.

Two smaller gaps are recorded in `POSITIONING.md` and unchanged: claiming is one wallet prompt per stream rather than one prompt for everything, and a recipient is not told up front what a stream needs on their side. Both cost conversions in the first week of a stream, which is exactly when a recipient decides whether to care.
