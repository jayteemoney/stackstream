# Team Update Email (ready to send)

**Subject:** New direction for StackStream, plus the refreshed content and marketing plan

Team,

Off the back of our check-in, I dug into where StackStream really wins and I want to lock our direction. Here is the short version, what I changed, and what each of us does next.

**The decision: we lead with "real-time money, settled on Bitcoin."**
Streaming mechanics are not our edge. Sablier, Superfluid, and Streamflow have streamed billions and the primitive is everywhere. The one thing none of them can claim is Bitcoin finality. After the Nakamoto upgrade, Stacks blocks settle in about 5 seconds and are as final as Bitcoin once confirmed, and through sBTC we can stream actual Bitcoin by the second. That sentence is ours alone, so it becomes our headline.

**Interoperability and privacy: real, but roadmap, not now.**
I researched both across the Stacks ecosystem so we tell the honest story.
- Cross-chain: the sBTC bridge to Wormhole is built but not yet live, and it moves tokens only, not arbitrary messages. So "stream on Bitcoin, pay out on another chain" is buildable soon by composing on that bridge, but not a thing we ship this quarter.
- Privacy: a project called Verifold has shown that zero-knowledge proof checking can run inside an ordinary Stacks contract with no network changes. That means private, shielded streams are genuinely possible on Stacks. It is early and needs real cryptographic review, so we treat it as our long-term moat, not a near-term promise.

Bottom line: we put both on the roadmap, design our contracts to be ready for them, and never stake a milestone on infrastructure that is not live.

**What I already shipped on the site.**
The whole dApp now sells the value, not the plumbing. New headline, a clear "who this is for" section covering DAOs, merchants, freelancers, and grant programs, a simple Create, Stream, Claim walkthrough, and a feature set that leads with Bitcoin settlement and ends on the cross-chain and privacy roadmap. The dashboard, earn pages, and site metadata carry the same message. The copy is shorter, plainer, and easy to read. I also corrected our block-time numbers across the docs to match the post-Nakamoto 5 second reality.

**The refreshed content and marketing plan.**
Same channels, sharper message. Our positioning line everywhere is "real-time money, settled on Bitcoin." Four pillars carry every post: Bitcoin-final, real-time, who it is for, and the roadmap moat. We rotate five content types each week: educate, prove with real on-chain streams, spotlight one user type, tease the vision, and build in public. X stays primary, Discord is where we convert DAOs, Telegram and LinkedIn build community and credibility, and we lean on Stacks-native channels like the Forum, Sigle, and community calls. The full plan is in `MARKETING_PLAN_V2.md`. Treat it as a working draft: it is subject to review and change by the team as we build along, so push back on anything.
