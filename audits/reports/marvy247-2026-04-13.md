## Findings

### LOW-2 — `claim`: authorization check after balance calculations

**Severity:** Low (code quality)
**Issue:** `(asserts! (is-eq caller recipient) ERR-NOT-RECIPIENT)` was placed *after* the `let` block computed `effective-elapsed`, `streamed`, `claimable`, and `claim-amount`. A non-recipient caller triggered unnecessary computation before being rejected. No fund exposure — Clarity reverts atomically — but auth-first ordering is best practice.

**Resolution:** Authorization moved before state reads in `claim`. See current `stream-manager.clar:333`.

**Triage classification:** Fixed-on-main (auth-first reorder applied).

---

### LOW-3 — `top-up-stream`: authorization check after arithmetic

**Severity:** Low (code quality)
**Issue:** Same pattern as LOW-2 in `top-up-stream`.

**Resolution:** Documented as Accepted-documented. The arithmetic in question is read-only (computes `additional-blocks`, `new-deposit`, `new-end-block` from inputs and current state); there is no state mutation before the authorization check. Reordering deferred — no exploitability.

**Triage classification:** Accepted-documented.

---

### LOW-4 — `resume-stream`: no end-block guard

**Severity:** Low
**Issue:** A sender could resume a paused stream after its `end-block` had passed, leaving status as `STATUS-ACTIVE` indefinitely. No extra tokens accrued (elapsed time was clamped at `end-block`), but the on-chain state was misleading and confused downstream readers.

**Resolution:** `(asserts! (< current-block (get end-block stream-data)) ERR-STREAM-ENDED)` added in `resume-stream`. See current `stream-manager.clar:451–453`.

**Triage classification:** Fixed-on-main.

---

### INFORMATIONAL-2 — `stream-factory.total-deposited` does not reflect top-ups

**Severity:** Informational
**Issue:** `track-stream` snapshots `deposit-amount` at tracking time. Subsequent top-ups and cancellations do not update factory analytics, so `total-deposited` drifts from on-chain reality.

**Resolution:** Documented as Accepted-documented. Off-chain indexer (Chainhook) is the canonical analytics source for v1; factory `track-stream` is intentionally a lightweight registration hook, not a real-time accumulator. See L-1 in FINDINGS_TRIAGE.md (duplicate finding).

**Triage classification:** Accepted-documented.
